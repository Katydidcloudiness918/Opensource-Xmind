# 🧠 Opensource-Xmind - Plan Smarter, Visualize Bigger

## 🚀 What Is Opensource-Xmind?

Opensource-Xmind is a **completely free, open-source mind mapping application** inspired by XMind. It helps you turn messy thoughts into clear, organized visual diagrams. Whether you're brainstorming ideas, planning a big project, or studying complex topics, this app gives you a simple and powerful canvas to map everything out.

Think of it as a digital whiteboard for your brain. You create "nodes" for ideas, connect them with lines, add colors, and rearrange everything until your thoughts make perfect sense. It's perfect for students, professionals, writers, managers, and anyone who wants to think more clearly.

The best part? Since it's **open-source**, the code is publicly available. That means it's constantly improved by a community of developers, and **you'll never pay a cent** for it. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no premium tiers. Just pure, unlimited mind mapping.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🗂️ Intuitive Node-Based Editing
Create parent nodes, child nodes, and sibling nodes with a single click or keyboard shortcut. Drag and drop anything anywhere. Resize, recolor, and reshape your ideas effortlessly.

### 🎨 Beautiful Visual Themes
Switch between multiple professionally designed themes. Choose from different color palettes, line styles, and background patterns to make your maps both informative and appealing.

### 📝 Rich Text Support
Add detailed notes to any node. Format text with bold, italics, bullet points, and highlights. This is great for attaching meeting notes, research data, or personal reminders.

### 🔗 Smart Connections
Besides parent-child links, you can draw free-form "floating" connections between any two nodes. Show relationships, dependencies, or contradictions with labeled arrows.

### 📂 Import and Export Flexibility
Open and save files in the popular `.xmind` format, as well as JSON and Markdown. You can share your mind maps with others even if they don't use this app.

### 🔍 Zoom and Pan
Zoom in for precise editing or zoom out for a bird's-eye view of your entire map. Pan smoothly across large canvases without losing your place.

### ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
Power users will love the extensive keyboard shortcuts. Create nodes, delete branches, fold and unfold sections, and navigate maps without ever touching the mouse.

### 💾 Auto-Save and Local Storage
Your work is automatically saved to your local computer. No cloud accounts, no internet connection required. Your private ideas stay private.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Opensource-Xmind really free forever?
**Yes, 100%.** There are no premium features, no trial periods, and no paid licenses. It is released under an open-source license, meaning the source code is free for anyone to use and modify.

### Will this app damage my computer or steal my data?
**No.** This is a community-driven open-source project. The code is publicly reviewed. Your mind maps are stored locally on your own hard drive. Nothing is sent to any server. There is no advertising software embedded in the application.

### I get a "Windows protected your PC" warning. What should I do?
This warning appears for many newly released apps that aren't yet known by Microsoft's database. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway." You only need to do this the first time.

### Can I use this app on a Mac or Linux computer?
The primary supported systems are Windows 10 and Windows 11. However, because it's open-source and built on web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript), future versions may support other operating systems. Check the GitHub page for current updates.

### How do I save my work?
Opensource-Xmind automatically saves your work as you type. However, you can also manually save anytime by clicking "File" > "Save" or pressing **Ctrl + S**. You can choose a location on your PC.

### Can I open mind maps created in XMind (the paid app) in Opensource-Xmind?
Yes, the app supports the `.xmind` file format. You can open existing XMind files and continue editing them here without loss of data.

## 💡 Tips for Productive Mind Mapping

- **Start with a Central Idea:** Place your main topic in the center of the canvas. Branch out from there.
- **Use Colors to Group Ideas:** Assign specific colors to different categories (e.g., blue for tasks, green for resources, red for risks).
- **Fold Unimportant Branches:** Collapse nodes you're not working on to reduce visual clutter.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts Save Time:** Memorize `Tab` to add a child, `Enter` to add a sibling, and `Delete` to remove a node.
- **Export to Share:** Use the export function to create a simple text outline if you need to share your map with someone who doesn't have the app.
